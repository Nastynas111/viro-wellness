How fast will I feel it?

Most men report sharper energy and clearer thinking within 7–21 days on the Foundation Stack. The deeper biological adaptations — sirtuin activity, mitochondrial density — compound over 3–6 months.

Is it safe with my medications?

VIRO formulas are dosed at clinically supported levels and third-party tested. We strongly recommend reviewing your stack with your physician if you're on prescription medication, especially SSRIs, MAOIs, or blood thinners.

Where are the products made?

All formulas are manufactured in FDA-registered cGMP facilities in the United States. Every batch is tested for identity, potency, and heavy metals by an independent third-party lab.

What's the difference between NMN and NAD+?

NMN is a direct precursor — it converts to NAD+ inside your cells, typically within 15 minutes. NAD+ supplements deliver the molecule itself via liposomal absorption. Most men benefit from both: NMN for the steady supply, NAD+ for the rapid boost.
