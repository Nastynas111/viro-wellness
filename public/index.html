FAQ

Honest answers.

Are these supplements FDA-approved?

Dietary supplements are not approved by the FDA in the way drugs are. VIRO formulas are manufactured in FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facilities in the United States. Every batch is tested for identity, potency, heavy metals, and microbials by independent third-party labs. COAs are available on request.

How fast will I feel something?

Most men report a noticeable lift in energy and clarity within 2 to 4 weeks. Recovery, sleep, and mood changes typically emerge by week 4 to 8. Hormonal and biological-age markers shift over a 90-day window.

Can I take these with my current medications?

Always consult your physician before adding supplements to a medication regimen — especially SSRIs, MAOIs, blood thinners, or thyroid medication. Methylene Blue in particular has known interactions and should never be combined with serotonergic drugs.
